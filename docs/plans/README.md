- **>50%** of active producers opt in to WhatsApp sale alerts
- **>30%** of producer-attributed sales have `src` of `whatsapp`, `instagram`, `profile`, or `embed` (bio/share loop working)
- **>10%** of exclusive/high-ticket sales close via an offer token rather than catalog price
